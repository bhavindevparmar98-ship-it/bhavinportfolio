## 🎯 Deploy in 3 Easy Steps

### Step 1: Fix GitHub Email Privacy (1 minute)
Go to: https://github.com/settings/emails

**Option A:** Make email public
- Find your primary email
- Uncheck "Keep my email addresses private"
- Save

**Option B:** Use GitHub's no-reply email (recommended)
- Copy your no-reply email: `YOUR-ID+noreply@github.com`
- It's shown in the same settings page

### Step 2: Connect to Vercel (2 minutes)
1. Go to https://vercel.com
2. Click "New Project"
3. Import from GitHub: `bhavindevparmar98-ship-it/bhavinportfolio`
4. Keep default settings
5. Click "Deploy"

**That's it!** Your site will be live at: https://bhavin-portfolio.vercel.app/

### Step 3: (Optional) Add Custom Domain
1. In Vercel dashboard, go to Settings → Domains
2. Add your custom domain
3. Update DNS records (Vercel will show instructions)
